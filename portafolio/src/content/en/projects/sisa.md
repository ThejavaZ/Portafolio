System built during my internship at **SISA Sistemas Automatizados (SISA&AUT)**. The main goal was to digitize and centralize the assignment of computer equipment to staff.

The application keeps a detailed history of maintenance, hardware status and equipment responsibilities, cutting the IT department's response times by **40%** by removing manual spreadsheets from the process.
